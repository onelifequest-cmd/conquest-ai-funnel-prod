A plan built around your business

April, you already have a strong foundation: a clear brand, a live site, online checkout, and a free consultation people can book. This plan is how we help you catch every one of those opportunities, follow up without adding hours to your day, and build systems that let Adore Credit Enterprise grow with you.

You stay in the lead. You still review each client’s file. You approve messages and posts in your Conquest AI dashboard (Mission Control) before anything goes out. We cover the phone, the follow-up, the partners, and the public proof around you.

What this means for you

These are the outcomes we are aiming for together. We do not promise a number of new clients, reviews, or credit-score results — and we will never market a guaranteed score change or timeline.

Answer every call, even after hours

Turn your Facebook audience into booked consults

Build your 5-star reviews

Partners who can send you clients

Phase 1 — Never miss a call

Phase 1

Your phone and inbox, covered

We start by catching every inbound opportunity and making the public path into your business simple and consistent. We work with the site and calendar you already have. This is not a website rebuild.

  1. Put Grace on the phone

    Your AI receptionist answers around the clock, texts people back, and books your free 30-minute consultation. She is trained to speak carefully about credit repair — no guaranteed results, no promised timelines.

  2. Open your CRM and your dashboard

    We set up your CRM for credit repair and open your Conquest AI dashboard (Mission Control). That is where you approve texts, posts, and outreach. Nothing goes out without your sign-off.

  3. Set up official business texting

    We start the registration your texts will need so missed-call replies, follow-up, and later campaigns can run the right way.

  4. Make your pricing consistent across every page

    We line up the prices people see on your site, in previews, and at checkout so every page tells the same story.

  5. Create one clear booking path

    We point your Facebook, Linktree, and shop links to the same consult offer Grace is booking, so a click and a phone call lead to the same next step.

  6. Plug into the tools you already use

    We learn how you work today, including any credit-repair software you already rely on, and we connect to it. If you use Credit Repair Cloud, we will hook it up. If you don’t, we leave your process as it is.

What you get

  • Calls answered even after hours, with a text back and a path to book your existing consult
  • A CRM and dashboard ready for follow-up, with you in control of what goes out
  • A cleaner, more consistent path from a post or a click to a booked conversation

Phase 2 — Follow every lead

Phase 2

No inquiry left sitting

Once the phone is covered, we make sure every consult, assessment, and checkout has a next step — and we start building the public proof your brand deserves.

  1. Map a simple path in your CRM

    From a booked consult, to your discovery assessment, to the membership or package that fits. People who start and do not finish still have a clear next step.

  2. Turn on thoughtful follow-up

    Automatic email and text sequences — once official texting is ready, and only with your approval — for missed calls, incomplete assessments, and unfinished checkout.

  3. Help you build 5-star reviews

    After real work is done, we send review requests you approve, and we help you stand up a Google Business Profile so that proof has a home.

  4. A steady content rhythm you approve

    We draft up to 30 social posts a month in your voice, rooted in the A.C.E. Method: Assess, Correct, Elevate. You approve every post in your dashboard. No score promises, and no language we should not use.

  5. Turn your personal brand into education

    We help you reshape the content that already connects with your Facebook audience into careful credit-education posts — and, if you want one, an online community for your clients.

  6. A daily growth-text program for your clients

    Short, useful texts that keep clients moving between appointments and bureau steps, with an easy way to pause or stop. You approve the program before it goes live.

  7. Draft partner referral pages in your brand

    Simple pages so a mortgage broker, realtor, or car dealer can send you a client in one step, ready to launch in Phase 3.

What you get

  • Follow-up that still happens when you are with a client
  • A clear path to gathering 5-star reviews from people you have already helped
  • A living content program in your voice, with every post under your approval

Phase 3 — Partners and systems

Phase 3

A business that can grow with you

With coverage and follow-up in place, we build a partner channel in the Dallas–Fort Worth market you already serve, and we set a weekly rhythm so the company can take on more without adding hours to your phone.

  1. Find referral partners and bring them into your CRM

    We look for mortgage brokers, realtors, and car dealers in Dallas–Fort Worth and add them as partners — not as consumer leads. You approve any outreach before it goes out.

  2. Launch your Refer-a-Client pages

    Partners get a simple form to send you someone. When that person calls, Grace can book the consult.

  3. Text campaigns to people who already know you

    Only to a list you own, only after official texting is ready, and only with your approval. We do not send cold texts to strangers.

  4. Give Adore Credit Enterprise a business home online

    Alongside your personal profile, we help you build a business presence where people can find the company, see your work, and leave a review.

  5. Reconnect with people who already started

    A careful follow-up sequence for earlier consults and unfinished assessments — no pressure copy, all approved by you.

  6. A weekly rhythm in your dashboard

    Your Conquest AI dashboard becomes the place you review a short list: posts, texts, and partner outreach. We bring you what needs a decision, not a flood.

What you get

  • A growing list of local partners, with outreach that only happens when you say yes
  • A way for those partners to send you a client in one step
  • A weekly operating rhythm that lets the business grow without stretching your phone hours
You stay in control

Every text, post, and partner message waits for you in your Conquest AI dashboard. Our scripts, pages, and posts stay careful: no promised score changes, no guaranteed removals, and no fixed timelines. Our marketing stays focused on your education, your repair services, and your partner referrals.
